Seat Relay

Every Bayern home game turns away more members than it seats, yet season-ticket seats still sit empty when holders cannot make it and never get round to listing them. Seat Relay uses Gemini to get those seats to the members who lost the ballot, and rewards the holder for passing them on.

Why it should work

90→96%Brentford season-ticket seat use after a release deadline and exchange credit, 2024/25.brentfordfc.com
13,000+Bundesliga holders in a field study: plain reminders failed, only a targeted, valuable incentive cut no-shows.essma.eu
350,000Ticket requests for one 75,000-seat home match against Dortmund, per the CEO in August 2026.sport1.de

Built to be fair and GDPR-safe. Opt-in only. The model runs on each holder's own scan history and only ever grants credit or upgrades; it never feeds cancellations or sanctions. No seat-neighbour or friend data is shown to anyone. Bayern publishes no no-show rate, so every number in the prototype is an illustrative example.

The data side. A no-show model on synthetic season-ticket data, with the targeting simulation and the Gemini prompt, is in the project repository. Independent concept by Rienhardt van Eeden, not affiliated with FC Bayern München or Google.
